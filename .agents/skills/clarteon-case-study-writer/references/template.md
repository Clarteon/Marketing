---
title: [Short title naming the problem]
slug: [slug]
type: case-study
status: draft
publishedAt: [YYYY-MM-DD, set on publish]
summary: [One or two plain sentences]
tags: [..]
synonyms: [..]
client: [Name, or "A [size] [kind of business]" if anonymous]
solution_type: [automation | AI agent | integration | custom software | website | mobile app | CRM]
permission: [Who approved, how, and when]
---

## The problem
[In the client's words, plain and specific.]

## What we did
[What was built or changed, and why this solution fit. Mention the diagnosis step.]

## The result
[Only supported results. If unmeasured, say what changed in plain terms.]

## In their words
[Optional short quote, only with permission.]
