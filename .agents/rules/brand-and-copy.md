# Brand and copy rules

## Visual identity

- Palette: Ink #0A0A0A (text, dark sections), Paper #FFFFFF (background), Stone #F4F4F2 (cards, quiet sections), Graphite #5C5C58 (secondary text), Lime #C6F432 (actions and highlights).
- Lime is a spotlight, never a wash. Use it only as a fill with Ink text on top. Never use lime as text on a light background, because the contrast is too low.
- Fonts: Fraunces for headlines, Inter for body and interface. Instrument Serif is the alternative headline face.
- Logo: wordmark "Clarteon" in Fraunces plus a small aperture symbol, a lime tile holding a "C" that opens like a lens around one point of focus. It must work at 16 px as a favicon.
- Feel: clean and corporate, but bold and minimal. Generous whitespace, one idea per section, few elements.
- Buttons: pill shape, at least 44 px tall (56 px for hero actions), primary is lime with Ink text, secondary is an Ink outline.

## Accessibility baseline

- Text contrast meets WCAG AA. Visible focus states on every interactive element. Touch targets at least 44 px.
- Every image has useful alt text or is marked decorative. Headings follow a logical order.

## Copy voice

- Name the problem before the product. Use plain words and short sentences.
- Say "discovery call" consistently.
- No unproven claims: no numbers, savings, awards or client names unless real and approved.
- Match Clara's voice: warm, plain-spoken, like a sharp colleague.
- Use typographic apostrophes and quotes in all visible text.

## Approved homepage copy

- Headline: Technology that starts with your problem.
- Subhead: Clarteon is the technology partner that believes the right solution starts with the right question. We find the repetitive work slowing your business down, then automate it or build what you need, so you can grow.
- Actions: Book a free discovery call, Send a project brief, Or message us.
- Section headings: "Tell us what is slowing you down." (problems), "Four steps. Clear and simple." (process), "Work you can look at." (proof), "Tell us the problem. We will find the clearest way through." (closing).

## Placeholders

Bracket placeholders such as [FOUNDER NAMES AND BIOS] must never ship. A build check must fail the production build if any `[ALL CAPS]` placeholder remains in rendered content.
