## Why Clarteon exists

Most technology projects start with a tool looking for a problem. Clarteon starts the other way around. We find the repetitive work or the broken handoff that is slowing a business down, then recommend the clearest way to fix it. Sometimes that is automation. Sometimes it is a chatbot, a piece of custom software, a website, or a connection between tools you already use.

## How we work with clients

We keep the first step small: a discovery call to understand the problem, then a plain-language recommendation. If the work is a fit, we build in short, visible steps and stay for the questions that only appear after launch.
