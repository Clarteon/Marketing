## What this preview collects

This preview does not connect Clara or the project brief form, so it does not collect the information you type into them. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes standard technical logs to serve the site and keep it secure, under its own policy.

## When Clara and the forms go live

Clara and the forms will collect the minimum needed to answer you: your name, email address, the business details you choose to share, and the messages you send. Messages will be processed by a third-party AI provider so Clara can reply. The booking flow will use a calendar provider to create the event and a Meet link.

This page will name each provider, explain why the data is processed, state how long it is kept, and explain how to request removal before those features are switched on.

## Your choices

You can ask for your information to be removed. A contact address for privacy requests will be published here before Clara and the forms go live.
