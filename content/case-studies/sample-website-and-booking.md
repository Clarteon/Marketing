## The problem

[CLIENT NAME] had a website that looked fine and did very little. Visitors landed on a page about the history of the company, then had to guess whether it handled their problem. The contact form asked for a budget before it asked what was wrong.

[FIRST NAME] put it this way: “People either did not write to us at all, or they wrote to us about work we do not do.”

## What we did

We read the last [NUMBER] enquiries and sorted them into the problems the business actually solves. In the diagnose step it became clear the site was answering a different question from the one visitors arrived with.

We rebuilt the pages around those problems, put the likely solution and a way to book next to each one, and replaced the contact form with a short project brief. [WHICH PAGES WERE KEPT, MERGED OR RETIRED].
