## The problem

A [SIZE]-person [KIND OF BUSINESS] took orders by email, typed them into a spreadsheet, then typed them again into the invoicing tool. [NUMBER] orders a week, three entries each. Most mistakes were caught at the packing table, after the customer had already been told the order was on its way.

[FIRST NAME], who runs operations, described it plainly: “[SHORT QUOTE IN THEIR WORDS, ONLY WITH PERMISSION]”.

## What we did

We started with a week of watching, not building. In the diagnose step we followed one order from the inbox to the invoice and wrote down every handoff. The orders were not complicated. The handoffs were.

The fix was an integration between the inbox, the order sheet and the invoicing tool, plus a short set of checks that stop an order before it moves on if something does not add up. No new system for the team to learn, because the system was never the problem.
