# Email types

Keep each email short, plain, and in Clarteon's voice. Do not invent facts. Use bracket placeholders only in drafts.

| Email | To | When | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New lead alert | Team inbox | After a lead is saved | Name, email, company, what they need, source (form or Clara), conversation summary if from Clara, timestamp |
| Project brief receipt | Visitor | After the form is saved | Thanks, what happens next, how to reach a person, reply-to the team inbox |
| Booking confirmation | Visitor and team calendar guest | After a call is booked | Date and time with timezone, Google Meet link, how to change the time (reply to the email), what to expect on the call |
| Solution brief | Visitor and team | After a Clara diagnosis, only if the visitor agreed | The problem in the visitor's words, the suggested direction, the likely solution type, the suggested next step. No prices and no promises |
| Failure alert | Team inbox | Failed booking, failed send, or usage threshold crossed | What failed, when, and the affected lead id (no personal data in the alert body) |
